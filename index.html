What it does

Al Burhan Asfar helps a group travelling together in one or more cars plan the trip, record shared expenses, attach receipts, and settle up fairly at the end. It also carries travel azkar and fiqh guidance for the road.

Features

  • Trips with stops, cars, members and guests
  • Expense logging with receipt photos and pool contributions
  • Automatic fair split and settlement summary per car
  • Audit history for every change
  • Admin portal for trip oversight and reports
