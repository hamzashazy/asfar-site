Who we are

Al Burhan Asfar is a private travel companion and trip expense app operated by Al Burhan for its members and invited guests. Contact: atfaalalburhan@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, mobile number and an optional profile photo, used to sign you in and identify you to other trip members.
  • Trip data: trips, stops, cars, members, expenses, payments and settlement records that you or other members of your trip enter.
  • Receipts: photos or PDF files you choose to attach to an expense.
  • Audit history: who changed what and when, kept so that shared money records can be trusted.
  • Device notifications: a push-notification token if you allow notifications.

Where it is stored

Account and trip data are stored in a Supabase database. Receipt files are stored in a Google Drive account that belongs to Al Burhan; the app accesses only the files it creates there, using Google's drive.file permission. Nothing is stored on third-party advertising services.

Who can see it

Your trip data and receipts are visible only to members of that trip and to Al Burhan administrators. We do not sell or share personal data with anyone else. Google processes receipt files only to store them, under Google's own privacy policy.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google Drive access is used solely to store and retrieve receipt files for expenses and for no other purpose.

Your choices

You can edit your profile in the app. To delete your account or any receipt, email atfaalalburhan@gmail.com and we will remove it; records needed to keep other members' settlement history accurate may be kept in anonymised form.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
